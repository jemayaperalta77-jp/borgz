About Us

Our Story

I started making that 'favorite burger' in my own kitchen for friends and family. Watching their faces light up told me I wasn't the only one looking for this. I opened Jem-Burger so I could finally share my personal gold standard with the world. I don't just sell these burgers; I’m still their biggest fan. Every time I flip a patty, I’m making it exactly the way I’d want to eat it myself.

Personal Passion

Our mission is to share the burger I spent a lifetime searching for—hand-crafted with obsession, served with pride, and designed to be the best meal of your day.

Quality

Fresh, Never Frozen Beef: Our patties are hand-pressed daily using 100% premium chuck. We never use additives, fillers, or preservatives because real flavor doesn't need them.
